### Assumptions made in this spec (root `CLAUDE.md` § How to Act #1)

1. **Pin formula (design erratum).** `design.md` § 2 states the pytest pin as
   `first_value_bars + _CROSSOVER_LOOKBACK == builtin_lookback_bars`. With the design's own values
   (SMA/BB = period, RSI/ATR = period + 1) and `analysis/app/services/warmup.py:83-89`
   (`max(0, period - 1) + _CROSSOVER_LOOKBACK`, `period + _CROSSOVER_LOOKBACK`) that equation is off by
   one (SMA 200: 201 ≠ 200). The values are the binding part (they are the product spec's P). Step 4
   pins `(first_value_bars - 1) + _CROSSOVER_LOOKBACK == builtin_lookback_bars`, which is debate D-7's
   definition ("first valid index + 1"). Basis: D-7, D-17. Arithmetic re-checked at `/sdd-review
   impl-spec` (2026-11-15) against `warmup.py:80-89`: SMA 200 → (200 − 1) + 1 = 200; RSI 14 →
   (15 − 1) + 1 = 15. The review raised no finding on it; it remains a deviation from the `design.md`
   text for the operator to see at the Step 4 gate.
2. **Config-read timeout value.** `design.md` § 4 says "a short timeout" and gives no number. Step 13
   adds `HISTORY_CONFIG_TIMEOUT_MS = 3_000` to `ui/src/lib/deadlines.ts`. Basis: the shortest existing
   deadline is 10 s (`deadlines.ts:3`), and the read blocks the first bars request. The number is this
   spec's proposal; confirm it at the Step 13 Phase-2 gate.
3. **Seed column list.** `design.md` § 3 says the seed follows `023_ui_performance_keys.up.sql:17-32`.
   Step 7 keeps 023's namespace, consuming service and `ON CONFLICT … DO NOTHING`, and adds the
   `updated_by` sentinel column from `048_webhook_fanout_callback_keys.up.sql:4-6`, because the ledger
   rule "a seed migration's down deletes only rows it can prove it wrote" (`fails.md`, 2026-11-10, 293)
   needs it. A homelab row an admin has set to 1000 then survives a rollback.
4. **`scripts/config-wiring.json` entries.** `design.md` § 9 says "the reader entry in
   `scripts/config-wiring.json`". Read as a `registry.receivers` entry (Step 11), never a `findings`
   entry for the read key (`fails.md`, 2026-11-10, 291). One temporary `seeded-unread` finding bridges
   Steps 7-11 (precedent: 338 Step 1). `scripts/config-wiring-stored.json` must be regenerated by
   Step 7 — the checker requires it (`scripts/check-config-wiring.mjs:713-717`). This closes O-29.
5. **Homelab launch is not a numbered step.** A step must pass its Verification on the feature-branch
   tree (F-05); the launch needs the deployed seed. See Step Dependencies.
6. **Chunk-width query.** No chunk-width SQL exists in the repo (`grep -n timescaledb_information
   docs/runbooks/*.md` → none). Step 17 writes one against `timescaledb_information.chunks`; it is new
   text, checked by the operator on first use.
