## Archive Synthesis — 2026-08-19 — /sdd-archiver

**What**: Shipped 4 new shadcn primitives (Switch, Slider, Collapsible, NavigationMenu) plus reuse of Badge/Table/AlertDialog/Tabs/ToggleGroup/Alert/Checkbox and one new shared FilterToolbar.tsx, covering the 22 "medium confidence" occurrences from the shadcn gap audit — 2 tranches (37 steps), stacked branch on sibling 120. Two primitives named in the plan did NOT ship as designed: Tabs for EnvModeSwitcher was reverted (role mismatch), and FR-9's Accordion became Collapsible (structural mismatch). FR-13 flipped from a design recommendation to its opposite by direct user override.

**Why (irrecoverable rationale)**: The whole audit-driven backlog (120/121/122/123) migrates visually-identical hand-rolled controls onto vendored primitives for consistency, not new capability — which is why FR-13's replace decision turned on "match the rest of the migration's primitive coverage," a product/consistency preference only a human principal could make, not a correctness call. It also explains why several primitive swaps were correctly refused: consistency does not override a primitive whose ARIA/DOM contract fights the actual interaction model.

**Rejected alternatives**: FilterToolbar as a `layout: 'header-badge'|'grid'` variant enum — collapses into "two components behind a switch," not real DRY; chose slot-based with page-chrome at each call site (already insights.md:1293). Forcing AccountSelector.tsx's 2px status dot through Badge — Badge's padded-text box model needs ~9 override classes to fake a bare dot vs the existing 6-class span; kept the hand-rolled span. FR-13 KEEP AS-IS (the round-1/2 self-run debate's converged recommendation) — lost to a Round 3 user override.

**Scars & gotchas**: Slider leaked its implicit `<label>` text onto Radix's hidden SliderBubbleInput, colliding with a sibling numeric mirror's aria-label, and Slider's own aria-label never reached the visible Thumb — real e2e regression, fixed by `<label>`→`<div>` and forwarding aria-label to Thumb. ui/table.tsx already renders its own data-slot="table-container" div with overflow-x-auto (table.tsx:9), so a manual overflow-x-auto wrapper double-wraps — dropped at both FR-11 sites. e2e locator ambiguity from shared components: CredentialFields renders "API Key" fields in two places at once — added stable data-testid={account-row-${id}} to scope. AlertDialog trigger button and its own AlertDialogCancel share the literal label "Cancel" at 2 of 5 FR-4 sites — flagged for the next spec touching those sites.

**Permanent deviations**: design said FR-13 KEEP AS-IS (leave nav hand-built) → shipped REPLACE with NavigationMenu on PlatformHeader.tsx + BottomTabBar.tsx (user overrode the self-run debate on consistency grounds in Round 3). design pt 5 said `NavigationMenuLink render={<Link/>}` → shipped `<NavigationMenuLink asChild>` (installed radix-ui@1.6.7 navigation-menu is a re-export of classic @radix-ui/react-navigation-menu@1.2.22 — forwardRef/asChild, zero render; already fails.md:864). product-spec/FR-5 named Tabs for EnvModeSwitcher → shipped plain `<Link>`/`<Badge>` (TabsTrigger hardcodes role="tab" which Slot merges over the child Link's role="link", breaking nav semantics; already fails.md:941). product-spec/FR-9 framed the LiveStrategiesPanel detail reveal as Accordion → shipped Collapsible (the detail panel is one shared panel below the whole table with a single selectedId, and AccordionContent can't render outside a `<table>` while its Item wraps a `<tr>`; NOT previously in the ledger).
