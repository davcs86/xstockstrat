## Rejected Alternatives

- **Maximum 400 for the budget** — below today's ~690 live pairs (full-fleet-per-cycle could never be configured, the overflow state never cleared); its pool rationale was wrong (serial evaluation).
- **Interval bounds `[1, 900]`** — 1 permits near back-to-back cycles and is the likeliest unit typo; 900 leaves no headroom against the 30-min heartbeat alert with an extrapolated per-pair cost.
- **Minimum-only bound** — a typo like 50000 gives ~100-minute cycles and trips the heartbeat alert; a typo guard is cheap and matches house style.
- **Seeding only the budget key** — leaves the sibling `eval_interval_seconds` defect unowned (fails.md "sibling must be explicitly filed"); the operator chose to seed both (`alert_throttle_seconds` is a deliberate omission).
- **Hard dependency "293 merges after 281 and 291"** — serializes a SEV-3 fix behind unrelated features; replaced by the soft renumber-at-merge rule.
- **`DO UPDATE` upsert seed** — `--declared-migration` exits 2 "shape not understood".
- **Separate migrations per key** — doubles the ordering hazard.
- **`(over_budget, budget)` tuple or pair-count latch state** — extra field adds nothing; pair-count state would re-warn on drift.
- **Rate/time-window throttle** — a new knob that still re-warns under steady state.
- **A "recovered" INFO line** — YAGNI; the per-cycle INFO `evaluated N/M` and the exported `analysis.live_loop.truncated_pairs` counter carry the state.
- **Static `node --test` SQL-text test for AC-3/4/7** — cannot observe trigger, audit or precedence behaviour; the real-Postgres harness exists.
- **Removing the dead `_lock` skip** — out of scope; breaks `TestLiveLoopHeartbeat`.
- **Setting `updated_by` on the metadata UPDATE** — would hide who set the operator's value; the doc's sentinel convention is read as "value writes".
- **Guarding the down on `updated_by = sentinel` too** — more precise but a third NNN-bearing literal to keep in sync; the value-only guard is acceptable.
